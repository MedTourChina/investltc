## Why a sequence, and not a list

Everything on this site is organised by product: channels, funds, bonds. But you cannot open them in
any order you like. Each step needs the one before it, and the chain runs:

> **Phone number → bank account → payment apps → payroll → tax registration → investment account →
> risk assessment → annual tax settlement**

Skip a link and the later steps do not fail with a clear message. They fail with a refusal at a
counter, which is why people conclude "foreigners can't do this here". They can; the order is just
not obvious.

## Week one: the two things everything else needs

**1. A SIM card in your own name.** Real-name registration with your passport, at a carrier branch.
This is genuinely first: the bank, the payment apps, the tax app and your employer's payroll all send
an SMS code to prove it is you. A number borrowed from a colleague will strand you later.

**2. A Class I bank account.** Bring your passport, your residence permit, proof of employment or
study, and proof of address.

At the counter:

- Ask for a **Class I (一类户)** account, not Class II. Class II has a daily cap of roughly CNY 10,000
  and your salary will hit it.
- Ask what your **daily transfer limit** is, and whether it can be raised later.
- Ask whether the branch handles **foreign passport holders** — some will decline because the staff
  have not done it before. A large branch in a central district, or your employer's payroll branch,
  solves this.

## Week one, in parallel: three questions for HR

Ask these in your first week, in writing if you can:

1. **Which bank does payroll use?** Salary rails are bank-specific. Choosing a different bank can mean
   a paper transfer every month.
2. **Did you enrol me in social insurance and the housing fund, and in which city?** The employer's
   matching contribution is part of your compensation, and it is worth checking it exists.
3. **Does the company operate the eight tax-free allowances, or should I claim the special
   deductions?** The two are mutually exclusive and fixed for the tax year — see
   [allowances or deductions](/explainers/foreign-tax-allowances).

## Weeks two to four: the tax app

Register the **个人所得税 app** in person at a 办税服务厅, with passport and residence permit. The
app supports foreign passports, but the first registration usually needs a counter visit.

Do this early. The reason is not the deadline — it is that the app is where you can see whether your
employer has actually reported your income. A missing month is much easier to fix while you still
work there.

## Weeks two to four: payment apps

Bind the card to **Alipay** and **WeChat Pay**. Start with small amounts, and watch the limits: a
Class II account, or a newly opened account with a low transfer cap, will decline everyday purchases
in ways that look like the app is broken.

## Month two: the investment accounts, in order of difficulty

| Order | Account | Where | Needed for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Fund account** | Your bank's app, sometimes a branch | Public funds, money-market funds, REITs sold off-exchange |
| 2 | **Securities account** | A broker, in person or by two-way video | A-shares, ETFs, exchange-traded bonds, reverse repos |

The fund account is the easy one and it unlocks most of the retail shelf. The securities account is
the one that needs an appointment, and it is the gate for anything exchange-traded — our
[eligibility checker](/check) treats it as a blocker rather than a suggestion for exactly that reason.

Then take the **risk assessment**. It is a questionnaire, and its result (C1–C5) decides which
products (R1–R5) you are allowed to buy. Foreign residents are often defaulted to the conservative
end; if you intend to buy anything above R1, ask to be reassessed rather than assuming you are
locked out. See [risk grades](/explainers/risk-grades).

## Month three: start with the boring three

| Product | What it is for | The catch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Money-market fund** | Parking cash | Fast redemption is capped, commonly CNY 10,000 a day |
| **Reverse repo** | Short-term cash, exchange-traded | Minimum lot differs by exchange |
| **Large-denomination CD or fixed deposit** | Locking money away | Early exit costs the interest |

None of these is a recommendation. They are the three products where a mistake is small enough to
learn from — which is the actual point of a first quarter.

## What not to do first

- **Do not open a brokerage account before a bank account.** It will be refused.
- **Do not convert money "so you can invest".** Conversion is an FX transaction with a purpose test;
  see [the USD 50,000 quota](/explainers/usd-50k-quota).
- **Do not use a "friend's broker" or an introducer.** Unlicensed intermediation is where people lose
  both money and legal standing.
- **Do not buy anything whose risk grade you cannot name.** If the person selling it cannot name it
  either, that is the answer.

## The annual rhythm

Once a year, March to June, the tax year settles up and refunds are common — see
[filing your annual tax reconciliation](/playbooks/annual-tax-settlement). Everything else on this
page happens once.
