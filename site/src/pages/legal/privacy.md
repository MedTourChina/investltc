## Short version

- No accounts, no sign-up, no email collection.
- No analytics trackers, no advertising pixels, no cross-site cookies.
- The eligibility check and every calculator run in your browser. Your answers are not sent anywhere.
- The only data that exists is what the hosting provider logs for security and delivery.

## The eligibility check

The answers you select stay in the page. They are encoded into the URL **only** when you press
“Show my options”, so that you can share or bookmark the result. That URL is not sent to a server by
us; it is a normal page URL. If you share it, you are sharing your answers.

## Hosting logs

The site is served by Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may process IP addresses, request paths and
timestamps for security, abuse prevention and delivery. That is standard infrastructure logging,
outside our control, and not linked to any identity we hold.

## Cookies

We do not set cookies. If that changes — for example if a privacy-friendly analytics tool is added —
this page will say so first, and the change will appear in [Updates](/updates).

## Contact

If you email us, we hold your email and whatever you send. We use it to answer you, and we do not add
you to a list. Ask us to delete it and we will.

## Children

The site is aimed at adults managing their own money. It is not directed at children.

## Changes

Material changes to this policy will be logged in [Updates](/updates) with a date.
