## Two numbers, one gate

When you buy a wealth product at a Chinese bank, two ratings have to line up:

- **The product's risk grade**, R1 to R5, disclosed in the prospectus and on the app.
- **Your investor risk tolerance**, assessed by a questionnaire the bank makes you fill in.

If your tolerance is lower than the product's grade, the bank's system blocks the purchase. This is not
advice it is giving you; it is a regulatory suitability check, and it is enforced in software.

## What the grades mean

| Grade | Label | Typical contents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | Low | Cash, deposits, money-market instruments |
| R2 | Medium-low | Short-duration bond funds, low-volatility wealth products |
| R3 | Medium | Balanced funds, mixed-asset products, some credit exposure |
| R4 | Medium-high | Equity-heavy funds, structured products, high-yield credit |
| R5 | High | Leveraged, derivative-linked or concentrated strategies |

The labels are not standardised across issuers in practice. One bank's R3 can be another's R4, so read
the underlying holdings rather than trusting the number in isolation.

## Your tolerance grade

The questionnaire asks about income, investment experience, horizon and how you would react to a 20%
drawdown. The bank records a result — usually C1 to C5 — and that becomes a ceiling on what you can buy
in that institution.

Two practical observations:

1. **The answers are checked against your profile.** Claiming five years of experience when your
   account is three months old can get the assessment invalidated, and the bank may re-run it.
2. **It can be re-assessed.** If your circumstances change, ask for a new questionnaire. The grade is
   not permanent, but it is not something to game either.

## Why foreigners hit this wall

A foreign resident often lands in R1–R2 territory regardless of actual experience, because:

- The questionnaire is Chinese-only at many branches, and staff may fill it in for you — conservatively.
- Overseas investment history is hard to evidence in a form the bank accepts.
- Some branches default foreign passport holders to the lowest tolerance band as a matter of policy.

The result is that a product you are technically eligible to buy is blocked by a grade you did not
choose. Ask to see the recorded answers before signing, and ask whether the branch can accept overseas
statements as evidence of experience.

## What the grade does not tell you

It says nothing about whether the product is any good, what it costs, or whether the issuer will honour
it. A R2 product from a weak issuer is still a R2 product from a weak issuer. The grade measures
volatility of net asset value, not credit risk, liquidity or fees.
