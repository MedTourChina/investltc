## The shape of this path

You are here on a **residence permit for family reunion (团聚) or personal affairs (私人事务)** — the
spouse or child of someone working or studying in China. You have no employment, therefore no payroll,
no income record and no tax filings of your own.

That single fact decides most of this page: the gate on securities accounts is **proof of income**, and
you do not have any in your own name.

## What you can do

| Step | Status | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SIM card | Yes | Passport, at a carrier branch |
| Bank account (Class I) | Yes | Passport, residence permit, and usually proof of address |
| Alipay / WeChat Pay | Yes | Bind the card |
| Fund account | Usually | Through the bank app; conservative risk grade |
| Securities account | **Depends** | Income proof is the obstacle; the household's documents may help |
| Housing fund / social insurance | No | Unless you take up employment yourself |
| Tax settlement | No | Unless you have income in your own name |

## The account that is not joint

China does not have joint bank accounts in the everyday Western sense. A household ends up with:

- An account in the working spouse's name, holding most of the money.
- An account in your name, which you can operate freely.
- No legal mechanism by which you operate theirs, except a **power of attorney (授权委托书)** under the
  bank's own procedure — and many banks will not accept one for investment products.

Practical consequences:

- Money your spouse transfers to your account is a **domestic transfer**, not income; it is not
  taxable, but it also does not build an income record.
- Buying investment products in your name using household money is fine; the **securities account**
  question is the one that needs an answer.

## The brokerage question

Brokers want to know where the money comes from and that you can be reached. With no employment, your
options are:

1. **Ask the broker directly** what they accept for a dependant — some accept the spouse's employment
   and income documents plus a marriage certificate.
2. **Use the fund account first**, which is easier and covers money-market funds, public funds and
   some bonds.
3. **Wait until you have income in your own name** — freelance work, a local employer, or a business
   you legally operate.

Do not present a spouse's employment certificate as your own. That is document fraud, and the
consequence is worse than the refusal.

## If you also work

A family reunion permit does not itself authorise work. To be employed you need the permit changed or
a work permit of your own. Once you have employment, this path becomes the
[work permit path](/paths/work-permit): payroll, social insurance, the housing fund and the annual
settlement all become yours.

## Household money crossing the border

If money comes in from abroad for the household, the recipient's own account and the purpose rules
apply. If you later leave, the money in your name can be remitted out with the documents — but money
in your spouse's name needs their cooperation, which is why the *account in whose name* question is
worth settling early rather than at the airport.

## The sequence

1. SIM card, Class I account in your own name (do not use your spouse's account for daily life — you
   will hit the "prove it is you" wall on every app).
2. Bind payments, test a small transfer.
3. Ask the broker what a dependant needs, before assuming the answer is no.
4. Open the fund account if you want to hold anything.
5. If you plan to work, start the permit change early — it can be slow.
