## The shape of this path

A Z visa converted into a **work permit and residence permit for work**, an employment contract, and
a payroll record. This is the path most of this site describes, because it is the one with the fewest
blockers: employment answers the income question that every counter asks.

## What you can do

| Step | Status | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SIM card | Yes | First, always |
| Bank account (Class I) | Yes | Ask payroll which bank |
| Alipay / WeChat Pay | Yes | Bind the domestic card |
| Fund account | Yes | Through the bank app |
| Securities account | Yes | In person or by two-way video, with employment proof |
| Housing fund | Conditional | Depends on the city enrolling foreign employees |
| Social insurance | Usually required | Personal pension account matters more than the pension |
| Annual tax settlement | Yes | March to June, usually a refund |

## The three things unique to this path

**1. The housing fund is part of your pay.** If your city enrols foreign employees and your employer
contributes, the matching share is real money in your name. If it does not, that is a compensation
gap worth knowing about. See
[housing fund and social insurance](/explainers/housing-fund-and-social-insurance).

**2. The allowance choice is worth real money.** Foreign employees may exempt a list of reimbursed
expenses **instead of** claiming the standard deductions — never both, and the choice is fixed for the
tax year. Ask payroll which route the company runs. See
[allowances or deductions](/explainers/foreign-tax-allowances).

**3. The settlement is annual and is usually a refund.** Nothing comes back unless you file. See
[filing your annual tax reconciliation](/playbooks/annual-tax-settlement).

## The sequence

1. **Week one** — SIM card, Class I account, three questions to HR (payroll bank, social insurance and
   housing fund, which tax route).
2. **Weeks two to four** — register the tax app in person, and check your employer has reported you.
3. **Month two** — fund account, then securities account, then the risk assessment. Ask to be
   reassessed if you are defaulted to R1 and intend to buy more than cash products.
4. **Month three** — start with products where a mistake is small; see [first thirty days](/start).
5. **Next March to June** — file the annual settlement, claim treaty relief if it applies, keep the
   receipt.

## The renewal clock

Your residence permit expires before your investment horizon does. Two consequences:

- **Renew early.** An expired permit freezes bank operations at exactly the wrong moment.
- **Keep the old and new permits.** Brokers and banks will ask for both when your ID details change.

If you change employer, your permit and your payroll bank both change, and the tax app needs to show
the new employer. Do that reconciliation in the month it happens, not a year later.

## What you still cannot do

Employment does not change the investment rules:

- You cannot exceed the **USD 50,000 annual conversion** convenience quota without documents and a
  permitted purpose.
- You cannot use the capital-account routes reserved for institutions (QFII, Northbound, CIBM).
- You cannot buy products your risk grade does not allow, however much you deposit.
