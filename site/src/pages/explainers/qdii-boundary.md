## The product that looks like an exit

A **QDII fund** is an onshore fund, sold in renminbi, that invests abroad under a quota granted to the
manager. You buy it from the same app as any other fund; the money stays inside China; the exposure
does not.

That is the point. QDII is a way to own foreign assets **without moving money across the border**. It
is a permitted exception to the capital account, granted to institutions and passed on through retail
products.

## Why it is not on the map

This site maps routes that put **money into China** or invest **RMB earned in China**. QDII is the
mirror image: renminbi that stays here, buying exposure that is not here.

Including it would blur the one distinction the site exists to make. So the map stops at the border,
and this page explains the thing just outside it.

## The variants you will meet

| Product | What it is | The catch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **QDII fund** | Onshore fund with an offshore mandate | Quota limits, and the fund can close to new money |
| **QDII ETF** | Exchange-traded version | Can trade at a premium to NAV when quota is scarce |
| **Mutual recognition fund (互认基金)** | Hong Kong fund registered for sale onshore | Limited shelf, different fee structure |
| **Cross-border ETF link** | Onshore ETF tracking an offshore index | Premium risk is the same |

The premium is the recurring trap: when quota is exhausted, a QDII ETF's price can drift well above
the value of what it holds, and the buyer pays the difference.

## Can a foreign resident buy one?

The same gate as any onshore fund: a fund account, which needs a bank account, which needs the
documents. There is no additional nationality rule in the product itself — but in practice some
platforms restrict account opening, and some funds close to new subscriptions.

If you can buy onshore funds, you can usually buy QDII funds. That does not make it an inbound
investment route; it makes it an onshore product with offshore content.

## The test that separates the two

Ask one question about any product: **where does the money sit?**

- Money sits in China and buys Chinese assets: on the map.
- Money sits in China and buys foreign assets: QDII — outside the map.
- Money leaves China to buy foreign assets: outside the scope of this site, and the part with the
  hardest rules.
