## Why this exists

Most English-language writing about investing in China answers the wrong question. It explains
markets, products and returns — while the reader is stuck one step earlier: *can I even open this?*

That question has a real, checkable answer. It depends on where you live, where the money sits, what
your residence status is, and which branch you walk into. Almost nobody documents it, because it is
tedious, local and changes often.

This site documents it.

## Who writes it

A small group of foreign residents in China, working on this in their own time. No company behind it,
no investors, no revenue. That is a constraint and a feature: there is nobody to pay, so there is
nobody to bias the matrix.

Names and faces will be published alongside the first field notes. Until the dataset has been tested
in person, a byline would imply more authority than the content currently earns.

## What it refuses to do

- Sell products, take commissions, or run affiliate links.
- Recommend a broker, a fund or a product.
- Publish expected returns, rankings or “best of” lists.
- Offer personalised advice, or pretend a page can replace a licensed professional.
- Help anyone move money in a way the rules do not permit.

## Status

This is a **prototype**. Nine channels, documented from public sources, zero field visits. The
structure — matrix, eligibility check, comparison, playbooks — is the product. The data is the work
that comes next.

See [Method](/methodology) for exactly how each record is graded, and [Updates](/updates) for what
has changed.
