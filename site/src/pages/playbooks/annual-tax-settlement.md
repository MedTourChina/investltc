## What the reconciliation is

Every month your employer withholds tax on your salary. Once a year, the totals are recomputed:
income from all sources, minus deductions, against what was already withheld. The difference is
refunded or paid.

For a foreign employee the usual outcome is a **refund**, because the withholding was calculated on
salary alone and the deductions arrive later.

## Do you have to file?

| Situation | File? |
| --- | --- |
| You want a refund | Yes — nothing comes back unless you claim it |
| You owe more than CNY 400 | Yes |
| Total comprehensive income under CNY 120,000 | Usually exempt, even if you owe |
| You are a non-resident (not tax resident) | No — withholding is final |
| You have income from two employers or freelance work | Yes, almost always |

The window is **1 March to 30 June** for the previous calendar year. Late filing brings a daily
surcharge on anything owed.

## The order of operations

1. **Get registered in the app.** The 个人所得税 app needs real-name verification. Foreign passports
   are supported, but the first registration usually requires a visit to a 办税服务厅 with your
   passport and residence permit. Do this in February, not June.
2. **Check the income record** (收入纳税明细). Every employer that paid you should appear. A missing
   employer is a problem to fix now, not at the counter.
3. **Decide the allowance question.** The eight tax-free allowances and the special deductions are
   **mutually exclusive** — see
   [allowances or deductions](/explainers/foreign-tax-allowances).
4. **Enter the deductions** (专项附加扣除) for the categories you are claiming, with the supporting
   details the app asks for.
5. **Claim treaty relief** if your country has a treaty and you qualify; this is where a certificate
   of tax residency from home is needed.
6. **Submit and choose the refund route.** The refund goes to a Chinese bank card in your own name.
7. **Keep the receipt** (申报记录) and the refund record. Your home country will want them for the
   foreign tax credit.

## The three things that go wrong

**The name.** Your app profile, your bank card and your tax record must agree. A middle name missing
from one of them is enough to block the refund.

**The bank card.** A refund to a closed or Class II account bounces and has to be re-requested. Use the
account you will still have in July.

**The double claim.** Claiming both the allowances and the special deductions looks fine in the app
and fails at the reconciliation. The correction is a repayment plus a late fee.

## Leaving the country mid-year

If you leave China during the year, file the departure settlement before you go, and keep the
clearance certificate. It is also what a bank may ask for when you remit a large balance out — see
[leaving China](/playbooks/leaving-china).

## What to keep

| Document | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Annual reconciliation record | Proof of what was declared |
| Refund or payment receipt | Proof of settlement |
| Invoices behind reimbursed allowances | The exemption depends on them |
| Certificate of tax residency | Treaty relief, and the home-country credit |
| Monthly payslips | Reconciling the withholding |

Five years is a reasonable default.
