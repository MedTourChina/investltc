## Why the ETF instead of a fund

An on-exchange ETF and a bank-sold fund can hold the same index. The differences that matter:

| | Exchange ETF | Bank fund |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entry | Securities account | Fund account via bank or platform |
| Cost | 0.15–0.6% a year, plus trading commission | 1–1.5% a year, plus subscription |
| Price you get | Live, with a spread | Once a day, at net asset value |
| Settlement | T+1 | T+1 to T+3 |
| Minimum | 100 units | Often CNY 1 |

If you already have a brokerage account, the ETF is usually the cheaper wrapper for the same exposure.

## Pick the index first

Order of decisions, and the order matters:

1. **What exposure.** Broad onshore equity (CSI 300, CSI 500, CSI 1000), a sector, bonds, or gold.
2. **Which index rule.** Two ETFs tracking the "same" index can differ in sampling and rebalancing.
3. **Then the fund.** Fee, size, tracking error, and the spread on the exchange.

The failure mode is starting at step 3 — choosing a product because a salesperson or an app featured it.

## Check the premium

For cross-border ETFs, price can detach from net asset value:

- When the quota is used up, subscription is suspended and the ETF trades at a premium.
- You can buy at 5–10% above the value of the underlying holdings without noticing.

Look at the intraday reference net asset value (IOPV) on the trading screen and compare it with the
traded price. If the premium is wide, wait or use a different vehicle.

## The mechanics

1. **Enable fund trading** on the securities account; some brokers require a signature.
2. **Search the code**, not the name — names change, codes do not.
3. **Trade in lots of 100 units** during the session; use a limit order if the book is thin.
4. **Check the settlement**: T+1 for equity ETFs, T+0 for many bond, gold and cross-border ones.
5. **Watch the size.** A fund under CNY 50 million can be at risk of liquidation.

## Costs you will actually pay

- **Commission**: 0.01–0.03%, often with a CNY 5 minimum — which dominates a small order.
- **Management and custody**: inside the fund, 0.15–0.6% a year.
- **Spread**: whatever the book gives you, sometimes 0.1%, sometimes much more on thin products.
- **Stamp duty**: not charged on ETF trades, unlike individual shares.

## What this playbook does not do

It does not tell you which index to hold, or whether equities belong in your portfolio at all. It tells
you how the plumbing works, so that the decision you make is about the exposure and not about the
onboarding.
